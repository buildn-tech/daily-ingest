On-device speech in Google AI Edge Gallery

fresh · primary source

Published 10 Nov 2026 · Android, iOS, desktop, web · ML Kit / MediaPipe / LiteRT

What: Google introduced offline ASR and TTS pipelines that work beside on-device small language models. The release highlights multilingual recognition, low-latency speech, open-weight model bundles, and sentence-synchronized playback.

Why: an offline voice loop reduces data exposure and makes degraded-connectivity workflows viable.

Use: prototype voice capture for field notes, client walkthroughs, language learning, and private artifact navigation. Keep transcription, retrieval, and playback local where feasible.

Caveat: device thermals, language quality, model downloads, and accessibility testing matter more than the demo path.

Next: test a 10-minute offline transcript on a target phone and measure latency, battery, and correction rate.

XR Language Teacher is the pattern

workflow pattern

Camera + speech + local multimodal inference + structured bilingual output + TTS turns a model into a situated tool. The design lesson is to make each stage inspectable and replaceable.

  • Capture stays local by default
  • Structured output controls speech
  • Sentence sync preserves attention
  • Model and voice are swappable
Start with one narrow offline workflow; do not ship a generic “voice assistant” first.
