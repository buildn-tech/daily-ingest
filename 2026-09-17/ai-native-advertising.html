OpenAI’s advertising launch points toward sponsored agents, natural-language campaign operations, optional copy adaptation, and CRM/commerce integrations. The practical opportunity is a clearly labeled handoff from discovery to action—with measurement and user control kept intact.

Sponsored Agents

What it is. Clearly identified business-sponsored agents let users ask product questions, explain preferences, and follow a link when ready.

Why it matters. Ads can become an interactive qualification surface, but trust depends on obvious sponsorship and separation from independent answers.

Buildn / client use. Design client campaign experiences that answer bounded questions and route qualified intent to a human or owned workflow.

  • Label sponsorship
  • Bound the agent
  • Preserve handoff
Next: Write the sponsored-agent disclosure and escalation rules before drafting any creative. Caveat: OpenAI says Sponsored Agents are being tested with select US advertisers.

Natural-language Ads Manager

Fresh todayworkflow
  • Published / updated: 2026-09-16
  • Source: OpenAI

What it is. Advertisers can use prompts to create, update, analyze campaigns, and turn a website or creative brief into a campaign.

Why it matters. The campaign workflow moves closer to an agent surface; review boundaries become part of the product design.

Buildn / client use. Offer a brief-to-campaign pipeline that generates drafts, evidence links, and approval checkpoints rather than auto-publishing.

  • Draft faster
  • Keep approval human
  • Attach evidence
Next: Prototype a brief schema with objective, audience, claims, exclusions, and approval owner. Caveat: AI suggestions remain optional and advertisers retain review/edit control in the announcement.
