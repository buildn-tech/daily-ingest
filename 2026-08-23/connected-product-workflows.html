Operator opportunity

product principleclient work
  • unit of value: completed intent
  • design primitive: connector + policy + confirmation
  • risk: silent side effects

What: Connected apps make “workflow surface” a product capability, not an integration checklist.

Why it matters: The best client automation will expose what the system can do, what it will do next, and who must approve.

Buildn use: Turn dossier actions into inspectable cards with links, status, and one-click handoff.

Caveat: Avoid building a generic assistant; anchor every integration to a measurable job.

Action: Write a one-page action contract for a single high-frequency Buildn workflow.
