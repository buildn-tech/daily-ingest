Task-shaped agents: from chat to work surfaces

The newest practical signal is not another chatbot. It is the shift from conversational access toward task-shaped surfaces, connected context, and domain-specific agent work. That is a useful design constraint for Buildn: expose a job, its evidence, and its approval points—not an empty prompt box.
