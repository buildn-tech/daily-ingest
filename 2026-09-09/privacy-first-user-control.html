Privacy-First User Control

The differentiator is not “AI everywhere”; it is an explicit control surface for when data leaves the device, who can reuse it, and how work survives the vendor.

Selected because: Coverage gap: after several agent-native web days, this lane returns to the user’s side of the boundary—choice, local-first ownership, and controllable AI exposure.

What it is

Firefox offers a single control to block current and future AI features, individual feature toggles, multiple chatbot providers, and Smart Window for user-approved context.

2. Browsers compete on privacy

  • source: https://blog.mozilla.org/netpolicy/2026/09/01/browsers-compete-on-privacy-when-the-operating-system-allows/

What it is

Mozilla argues that mobile OS and distribution rules can limit independent browsers’ ability to provide extensions and privacy features, illustrated by Firefox’s iOS content blocker.

Buildn / client use

Why useful now

Create a capability matrix for web, desktop, iOS, and Android before promising privacy parity.

Caveat

Policy and capability differ by jurisdiction and platform; an in-browser feature is not a substitute for an ecosystem.
