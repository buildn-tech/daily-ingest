Towards a science of scaling agent systems

research2026-01-28
  • Source: https://research.google/blog/towards-a-science-of-scaling-agent-systems-when-and-why-agent-systems-work/

What: Google evaluates 180 agent configurations across five architectures and finds coordination helps parallelizable tasks but harms sequential ones.

Why it matters: “More agents” is not a design principle; task structure should choose the architecture.

Buildn/client use: Route Buildn work by decomposability: parallel source collection, single-threaded synthesis, explicit tool budget.

Caveat: Benchmarks are informative, not a substitute for a client’s workflow trace.

Follow-up: Label ten recurring tasks as parallel, sequential, or mixed and test the routing rule.

ARE: scaling up agent environments and evaluations

platform/paper2025-09-22
  • Source: https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/are-scaling-up-agent-environments-and-evaluations/

What: Meta Agents Research Environments provides configurable rules, tools, content, verifiers, and asynchronous Gaia2 evaluation.

Why it matters: Static benchmarks miss timing, changing state, ambiguity, and coordination failures.

Buildn/client use: Design Buildn evals as small environments with changing inputs and explicit verifiers, not only prompt-response tests.

Caveat: The research platform may require substantial setup and its benchmark is not a universal score.

Follow-up: Add one asynchronous task to the next agent regression suite.

  • Clear next experiment

AIRA₂: Overcoming Bottlenecks in AI Research Agents

research2026-04-16
  • Source: https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/aira-overcoming-bottlenecks-in-ai-research-agents/

What: AIRA₂ combines asynchronous multi-GPU workers, hidden consistent evaluation, and interactive ReAct operators.

Why it matters: Throughput, evaluation noise, and rigid operators are separate bottlenecks that compound over long searches.

Buildn/client use: Separate worker throughput, evaluator integrity, and operator adaptability in research automation proposals.

Caveat: Reported gains are benchmark-specific and depend on compute access.

Follow-up: Instrument a research workflow with independent worker, evaluator, and repair metrics.

  • Practical signal over hype
  • Clear next experiment

OpenAI Agents API

product release2026-09-20
  • Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api/

What: A managed Codex harness provides sessions, environments, subagents, files, tools, and long-running execution through an API.

Why it matters: The harness becomes a deployable primitive; teams can own UX and compute while outsourcing orchestration mechanics.

Buildn/client use: Compare managed harness economics against Hermes/Buildn orchestration for bounded client pilots.

Caveat: Public beta and provider coupling require portability and exit criteria.

Follow-up: Build a 30-minute spike that saves evidence and outputs to a known workspace.
