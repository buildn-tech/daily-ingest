Cisco AI Defense 26.9.3

release notes2026-09-16
  • Source: https://securitydocs.cisco.com/docs/ai-def/user/168305.dita

What: Cisco retires a coarse toxicity rule in favor of granular safety guardrails and improves MCP security profile actions and license visibility.

Why it matters: Policy systems are moving toward specific, inspectable rules and operational cost visibility.

Buildn/client use: Replace vague safety labels with named harms, scopes, thresholds, owners, and test cases.

GitHub local sandboxing

developer security2026-09-23
  • Source: https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-23-local-sandboxing-in-the-github-copilot-app

What: Copilot app agents can be restricted from files, networks, and credentials.

Why it matters: Least privilege is finally visible as a normal agent setting rather than an infrastructure-only concern.

Buildn/client use: Make capability grants explicit in every Buildn worker manifest.

Caveat: Local sandbox controls do not cover remote or external side effects.

Follow-up: Create a capability matrix for read, write, network, secret, and publish actions.

Require proof of presence for high-impact actions

access control2026-09-24
  • Source: https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-24-require-proof-of-presence-for-high-impact-actions

What: GitHub adds a presence requirement for high-impact Copilot actions.

Why it matters: High-impact automation needs a human signal at the moment of consequence, not only at setup.

Buildn/client use: Use presence/approval receipts for publishing, deleting, spending, or contacting external parties.

Caveat: Presence proves interaction, not informed judgment.

Follow-up: List Buildn actions that need presence and define a reversible fallback.
