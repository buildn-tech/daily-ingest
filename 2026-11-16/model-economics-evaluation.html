Dossier cards

Production routing is becoming a portfolio problem

strong signalmodel economics
  • source: vercel.com

What: Vercel’s November index reports production routing across more than 100T tokens, with decision models taking a meaningful share of requests.

Why it matters: Model selection is an operating policy: route by task value, latency, risk, and observed success—not benchmark rank.

  • Log cost per successful outcome, not tokens alone.
  • Try next: Build a 3-tier router: cheap default, specialist, escalation model.
Caveat: Gateway aggregates are vendor-reported and may not represent your workload mix.

Multimodal work needs its own benchmark

strong signalevaluation
  • source: blog.google

What: Google and Artificial Analysis introduced MKA for agents reasoning across visually complex files and producing knowledge-work outcomes.

Why it matters: Document-heavy client work fails in the gap between text QA and artifact completion.

  • Evaluate on source files, intermediate evidence, and final artifact quality.
  • Try next: Create five local tasks using PDFs, charts, and a required structured deliverable.
Caveat: Provider-created benchmarks can overfit their own capabilities and task framing.

AI R&D acceleration raises the value of measurement

watchresearch signal
  • source: metr.org

What: METR estimates Anthropic’s capability progress on well-specified tasks accelerated to roughly twice its earlier rate, while warning that much evidence is redacted.

Why it matters: If research loops compress, reliable internal evaluation becomes a strategic control surface.

  • Pair every automation with a longitudinal baseline and uncertainty log.
  • Try next: Treat acceleration claims as a reason to improve measurement, not as a forecast.
Caveat: The headline estimate is uncertain and public evidence is incomplete.

Third-party evaluation is moving toward real work

strong signalbenchmark hygiene
  • source: www.vals.ai

What: Vals AI maintains task-oriented evaluations spanning finance, science, medicine, and multimodal work with a consistent harness.

Why it matters: External harnesses can expose the difference between provider claims and repeatable task performance.

  • Use common prompts and cost-normalized scores when comparing models.
  • Try next: Select one external benchmark and reproduce its harness locally.
Caveat: Benchmarks still omit your data, policies, and failure costs.
