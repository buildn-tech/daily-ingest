Four adaptive lanes selected for freshness, practical leverage, and coverage gaps.

Executive readout

Today’s strongest signal is convergence: agent platforms are adding durable state, delegated authority, cost controls, and design-to-code seams. The practical Buildn edge is to keep those seams inspectable and portable—capture the principal, tool contract, model cost, evidence, and human decision rather than outsourcing the system of record.

Selection rationale

Platform durability earned inclusion because a major hosted workflow product is entering a deprecation window. Model economics earned inclusion because production routing and multimodal evaluation make “best model” an insufficient decision rule. Agentic web and commerce earned inclusion because delegated identity and payment controls are becoming concrete. Design-to-delivery earned inclusion as a coverage gap with direct client leverage and local-first portability.
