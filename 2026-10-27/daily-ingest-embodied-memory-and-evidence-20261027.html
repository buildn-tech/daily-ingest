MeMoREval benchmark

selectedpaper
  • source: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.12561v1

What it is. A 53-task protocol separates an experience phase from later information or action requests, with no new observations to hide memory failures.

Why it matters. This is a clean template for testing Buildn memory and report continuity without letting the agent search again.

Try next. Create a small evidence-recall benchmark for client facts, decisions, and source changes.

Caveat: Embodied tasks may not map directly to knowledge work; adapt the protocol, not the scores.

Frames plus scene graphs

selectedfinding
  • source: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.12561v1#S6

What it is. Across tested models, storing camera frames with scene graphs produced the highest aggregate success; descriptions alone were not a universal substitute.

Why it matters. For Buildn, preserve source artifacts plus structured claims instead of flattening everything into summaries.

Try next. Keep the original evidence link beside extracted facts and decisions.

Caveat: Best modality is task- and model-dependent; do not generalize to every workflow.

Open-loop action requests

selectedmethod
  • source: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.12561v1#S4.F1

What it is. The request phase executes a complete plan without new observations or replanning, isolating memory from exploration ability.

Why it matters. Client automations should have at least one “cold context” test to expose hidden dependence on recent chat state.

Try next. Replay a task from only its durable packet and compare the result.

Caveat: Open-loop tests are deliberately harsher than normal interactive operation.

  • Bounded action over vague autonomy

Inference cost as a memory metric

selectedfinding
  • source: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.12561v1#S5

What it is. MeMoREval measures both write-time and read-time inference cost alongside task success.

Why it matters. A memory feature that improves recall but doubles retrieval cost may be a poor product default.

Try next. Add cost, latency, and success columns to Buildn memory experiments.

Caveat: Cost depends on model, backend, and retention strategy.
