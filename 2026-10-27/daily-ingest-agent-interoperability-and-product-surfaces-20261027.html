Notion connected 160 apps in three weeks

selectedcase study
  • source: https://www.notion.com/blog/how-we-connected-notion-ai-to-160-new-apps-in-three-weeks

What it is. Notion used an agent-browser-backed Kanban pipeline to move MCP integrations from vetting through production verification, growing from 25 to 185 connectors.

Why it matters. The reusable pattern is a durable queue with checkpoints, not the headline connector count.

Try next. Model Buildn integrations as cards with prerequisites, evidence, and escalation states.

Caveat: Partner review, visual polish, and security decisions stayed human-owned.
  • Evidence before enthusiasm
  • Bounded action over vague autonomy

Figma Weave models via Figma MCP

selectedproduct update
  • source: https://www.figma.com/release-notes/

What it is. Figma’s October 26 release exposes Weave video and image models to external agents through Figma MCP, with credit consumption attached.

Why it matters. Creative agents now need both capability routing and spend visibility at the tool boundary.

Try next. Test a small client asset workflow with explicit model, credit, and approval metadata.

Caveat: Availability and costs depend on account, model, and Weave credits.
  • Evidence before enthusiasm
  • Bounded action over vague autonomy

Agentic Resource Discovery specification

selectedspecification
  • source: https://developers.googleblog.com/announcing-the-agentic-resource-discovery-specification/

What it is. Google’s new discovery specification targets a standard way for agents to find governed resources and capabilities.

Why it matters. Discovery is a product surface: descriptions, ownership, policy, and freshness determine whether tools are safely usable.

Try next. Create a Buildn manifest for report inputs, tools, owners, and allowed actions.

Caveat: Early specification; do not assume ecosystem compatibility yet.
  • Evidence before enthusiasm
  • Bounded action over vague autonomy

A2A moves to the Linux Foundation

selectedecosystem update
  • source: https://developers.googleblog.com/en/google-cloud-donates-a2a-to-linux-foundation/

What it is. A2A’s governance is broadening through a Linux Foundation structure with major cloud participants.

Why it matters. Transport and task handoff standards may become more portable, but trust and state semantics remain local design work.

Try next. Track A2A as an adapter target, not as the core workflow model.

Caveat: Interoperability does not solve identity, authorization, or outcome verification.
  • Evidence before enthusiasm
  • Bounded action over vague autonomy

ADK for Kotlin 1.0

selectedrelease
  • source: https://developers.googleblog.com/announcing-adk-for-kotlin-10-building-production-ready-ai-agents-in-kotlin-android-and-beyond/

What it is. Google’s Kotlin ADK reaches 1.0 for production-ready agents across Kotlin, Android, and beyond.

Why it matters. The client surface is widening: agent workflows can live closer to mobile and native product contexts.

Try next. Watch for a small Android companion surface to Buildn artifacts or approvals.

Caveat: A stable SDK does not remove deployment, consent, or evaluation work.
  • Evidence before enthusiasm
  • Bounded action over vague autonomy
