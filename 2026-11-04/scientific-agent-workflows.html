Scientific Agent Workflows

Scientific agents earn trust when they turn massive search spaces into ranked next experiments with provenance, not when they merely summarize papers. A fresh biology deployment shows the strongest agent signal is not a bigger chatbot but parallel, evidence-producing investigation over structured data.

Signal map

Selection rule: fresh primary-source signal, practical leverage, and a meaningful gap from the recent archive.

Thousands of agents analyze whole-brain CRISPR data

selectedresearch2026-10-21
  • source: https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-research-for-complex-biological-data/

What it is

OpenAI, PerturbAI, and Scripps used thousands of agents to analyze millions of mouse brain cells, with each agent examining a perturbation, literature, and possible explanations.

Why it matters

It is a concrete parallel-research pattern: partition a large search space, preserve intermediate findings, then identify promising hypotheses.

Buildn / client use

Adapt the pattern to client discovery, competitive research, or repository archaeology with explicit evidence bundles.

Useful now: The reported 32-minute analysis contrasts with months-to-a-year of serial human work.
Caveat: The result is a research lead, not validated biology; human experiments and domain review remain decisive.
  • Next: Prototype a 20-item parallel investigation with a shared schema for claim, source, confidence, and next test.

selectedcase study2026-10-14

What it is

Third Moment Research uses large parallel agent populations, Codex, and frontier models for long-running computational investigations.

Why it matters

Long-horizon work needs scheduling, checkpointing, experiment selection, and cost controls more than conversational polish.

Buildn / client use

Borrow the loop for Buildn: generate candidate analyses, run bounded backtests, compare evidence, and escalate only high-value branches.

Useful now: The case makes agent fleet economics and elapsed-time measurement visible.
Caveat: It is a customer story with specialized workloads; transfer claims require local baselines.
  • Next: Design a small budgeted experiment with stop conditions and resumable artifacts.
