Open AI Practice: Openness, Context, Output

Today’s open-AI signal is unusually operational. Mozilla argues that openness is a gradient across data, code, weights, and documentation; OpenAI’s Stampli case shows context-connected agents compressing launch work; APQC supplies the missing discipline: measure knowledge maturity and reuse, not content volume. Together they suggest a practical Buildn rule—make inputs inspectable, keep review human, and measure cycle-time outcomes.

Signal map

Executive read: “open” is useful only when the boundary is explicit and the resulting work remains inspectable.

Mozilla framework for openness

Fresh · Aug 20

What: A framework that evaluates openness across training data, code, weights, documentation, and surrounding governance.

Why: It replaces vague open/closed labels with a component-level vocabulary.

Use: Add an openness profile to model/vendor selection: inspect, use, modify, audit, govern.

Caveat: Openness does not automatically mean safer deployment.

Next: Score two candidate models across five layers.

Framework research paper

Primary research

What: The underlying Communications of the ACM work developed with academic, industry, and civil-society participants.

Why: It gives policy and engineering conversations shared terms for tradeoffs.

Use: Cite it in client procurement and governance briefs.

Caveat: A taxonomy still needs deployment-specific risk analysis.

Next: Separate material availability from permission to use it.

Stampli launch workflow

Fresh · Aug 20

What: Stampli reports reducing modeled launch production from 243 to 77 role-hours using Codex and ChatGPT Work.

Why: The leverage came from connected product context, notes, and decisions—not isolated copy generation.

Use: Pilot a source-linked client launch packet with human approval at every customer-facing boundary.

Caveat: Customer stories are directional and use modeled comparisons.

Next: Measure one real workflow baseline before adopting the headline multiplier.

APQC 2026 KM priorities

Measurement

What: Survey research on KM maturity, AI integration, priorities, threats, and future skills.

Why: It keeps “knowledge leverage” tied to maturity and business outcomes.

Use: Track freshness, reuse, findability, and time-to-decision for Buildn artifacts.

Caveat: Survey findings need local baselines.

Next: Define three knowledge metrics for the next engagement.

Mozilla principles

Values anchor

What: Mozilla’s broader principles for an internet that is open, accessible, and shaped by people.

Why: Technical openness has product and governance consequences.

Use: Use as a discussion prompt when choosing hosted versus local-first systems.

Caveat: Principles do not substitute for a threat model or operating budget.

Next: Write the user control and exit criteria for any AI pilot.
