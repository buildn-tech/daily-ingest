CWE-bench

paperfresh signal
  • source: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17020v1

What it is

A defensive benchmark uses 120 private repository tasks across 73 CWE types and all OWASP Top 10:2025 categories, requiring secure patches that preserve functionality.

Why it matters

Pass@1 alone hides coverage, retry gains, language gaps, and cost differences.

SkillRelayBench: cross-session artifact attacks

paperfresh signal
  • source: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.13227v1

What it is

This work evaluates how persistent artifacts can carry attacks across otherwise reset agent sessions and compares modern coding-agent harnesses.

Why it matters

Resetting chat history does not reset the environment; the filesystem can become memory with side effects.

Buildn / client use

EvolvAttack

paperfresh signal
  • source: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.15244v1
  • follow-up: Require review and provenance for every learned rule promoted to durable state.

What it is

A study of backdooring self-evolving vision-language agents highlights risk in systems that continually update memory and behavior.

Why it matters

Self-improvement expands the attack surface because a compromised update can persist.

Buildn / client use
