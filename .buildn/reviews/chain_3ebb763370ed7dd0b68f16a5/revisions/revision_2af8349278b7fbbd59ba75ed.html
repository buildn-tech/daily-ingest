GitHub Apps narrow permissions

fresh signalleast privilege

What it is. New repository-creation and installation-repository permissions reduce dependence on broad administration scopes; expiring access and workload federation are part of the direction.

Why it matters. Use GitHub Apps instead of classic PATs for agent integrations. This creates cleaner audit trails and smaller blast radius.

  • Buildn/client: Inventory integrations; replace the broadest PAT first.
  • Useful now: direct, testable change
  • Caveat: validate claims on your own task mix

Managed settings can fail closed

fresh signalgovernance

What it is. GitHub adds forceRemoteSettingsRefresh and minimum client-version enforcement for sandbox governance keys.

Why it matters. A policy that is only eventually applied is not a control. This is a practical template for agent runtimes that must refuse stale configuration.

  • Buildn/client: Add a “policy freshness” check before any privileged tool call.
  • Useful now: direct, testable change
  • Caveat: validate claims on your own task mix

Representation can change security results

fresh signalresearch

What it is. Threat-Preserving Representation Sensitivity evaluates how representation choices affect agent-security benchmark outcomes across ASB, MCPTox, and AgentDojo.

Why it matters. Benchmark scores can move because of encoding and representation, not just model safety. Freeze representations in internal evals.

  • Buildn/client: Store raw prompts, normalized prompts, and tool traces for every regression.
