Contextual AI for Physical-World Work

In physical-world and professional workflows, “close enough” is not a product strategy. The differentiator is grounded context, reversible steps, and evidence that survives handoff.
Editorial readout: A quieter but strategically useful lane: current product signals are moving AI from generic generation toward context-rich, high-consequence workflows in engineering, science, and enterprise work. It is under-covered in the recent archive and offers a sharper client-discovery lens.
