All-lanes top three

  1. Prototype read-only background work: start with a source scan that produces cited evidence and asks before writing.
  2. Harden browser-agent defaults: no JavaScript evaluation, scoped filesystem roots, pinned plugins, and telemetry declarations.
  3. Qualify high-consequence automation: map domain context, failure cost, validation, owner, and stop condition before promising autonomy.
