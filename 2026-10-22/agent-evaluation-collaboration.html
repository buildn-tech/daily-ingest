Kolab: benchmark the handoff, not just the task

HIGH LEVERAGEresearch · 15 Oct
  • Signal: 115+ hours · 50 professionals · 508 workflow steps
  • Use now: model whether a person will adopt, edit, or reject an output

What it is

A multimodal benchmark of real professionals using agents across 40 occupations, 10 sectors, and 90+ applications.

Why it matters

Autonomous completion scores miss the moment where work leaves the chat and enters a real workflow.

  • Add adoption/edit/reject labels to run traces.
  • Measure downstream quality, not only task success.
  • Next: Prototype a review-packet feedback schema.
Caveat: The dataset uses recorded workflows and may not transfer cleanly to every client domain.

cxbench: outcome pass can still be unsafe

MUST ADOPTresearch · 19 Oct
  • Signal: 69.29% outcome pass vs 13.81% outcome + process pass
  • Use now: regulated customer workflows and approval gates

What it is

A benchmark for six regulated customer-experience domains that separately scores business outcome and process compliance.

Why it matters

55.48% of runs were false-passes: the record looked right while the sequence, disclosure, or authority was wrong.

  • Store process evidence beside final state.
  • Make approval and disclosure checks deterministic.
  • Next: Add a false-pass metric to client pilots.
Caveat: Its regulatory examples are benchmark-specific; map rules before reusing them.

SkillForgeBench: strict completion exposes the gap

BENCHMARKresearch · 10 Oct
  • Signal: 842 tasks · five domains · strict top score 40.9%
  • Use now: evaluate skills, runbooks, and long-horizon agent jobs

What it is

A skill-grounded benchmark that derives tasks and rubrics from procedural skills, then scores both loose rubric items and all-constraint completion.

Why it matters

Plausible deliverables hide failures in procedure, verification, formatting, and scope discipline.

  • Separate loose quality from strict readiness.
  • Test empirical verification loops explicitly.
  • Next: Build a 10-task internal skill regression suite.
Caveat: Benchmark task construction is synthetic, so validate with real work artifacts.
