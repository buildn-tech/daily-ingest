Design the control plane, then the capability

Today’s adaptive selection moves across three under-covered surfaces: identity decisions for agent tools, practical open-source supply-chain readiness, and intelligence that lives closer to the device or physical system. The common thread is operational clarity: who may act, what evidence supports release, and what remains useful when the cloud is not the whole environment.

All-lanes top three

  2. Inventory five dependencies and assign a CRA role plus evidence owner to each.
  3. Add degraded mode to one workflow: what is cached, queued, or safely unavailable?

Selection rationale

Agent identity earns inclusion because a fresh MCP authorization pattern makes policy enforcement concrete. Open-source readiness fills a recent coverage gap and has practical deadlines without repeating the latest runtime-safety lane. On-device and physical systems diversify beyond software-only agent coverage while still yielding an immediately useful workflow design pattern.
