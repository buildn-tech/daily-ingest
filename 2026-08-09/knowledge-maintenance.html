What it is. A current PKM framework that defines a higher bar: capture meetings and documents from actual work, update existing notes instead of spawning duplicates, and keep knowledge current.

Why it matters. Stale context is an operational defect. A beautiful archive that cannot distinguish a superseded decision from a live one is weak input for both humans and agents.

Buildn/client use. Model coverage ledgers, decision records, and client briefs as maintained entities with last-reviewed dates, source links, and explicit supersession.

Useful now. It aligns directly with the adaptive ingest ledger: novelty is not just a new URL; it is a maintained claim about what changed.

Caveat: This is a vendor-produced comparison, so use the operating principles rather than treating Tana as the default tool.
  • Prefer updates over duplicates.
  • Attach provenance to claims.
  • Review high-value records on cadence.

Follow-up: Add last_reviewed, supersedes, and confidence to one recurring Buildn knowledge object.
