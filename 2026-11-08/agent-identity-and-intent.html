Agent Identity + Intent

The durable design pattern is an agent registry plus an action-time decision: who owns this agent, what purpose was declared, what authority is needed now, and how can access be revoked? This is the control-plane shape worth carrying into Buildn and client architecture reviews.

Signal map

Identity remains a coverage gap only when framed as policy at the moment of action: new governance material makes intent, ownership, delegation, and revocation more concrete than static inventory alone.

Agent Identity Governance Framework

selectedframework
  • Source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/agentic/agentic-identity-governance-framework-v1/

What it is

The draft treats agents as first-class identity subjects with cryptographic authentication, just-in-time scope, constrained delegation, human sponsorship, and complete decommissioning.

Why it matters

Use its lifecycle vocabulary to structure a client readiness workshop and a Buildn access review.

Why useful now

Fresh or newly actionable signal for a bounded experiment.
Caveat: It is a draft framework with survey statistics and should not replace threat modeling or legal review.

Follow-up

Score one agent against owner, identity, JIT access, delegation chain, telemetry, and deletion.
