Frontier training security becomes an operating discipline

  • Source: https://openai.com/index/securing-our-frontier-model-training-environment/

What: OpenAI describes defenses and operational changes for resuming frontier training after a security event.

Why it matters: Security posture is now part of delivery velocity: protected training, access segmentation, and incident learning affect whether capability work can continue.

Buildn/client use: Turn client AI readiness reviews into a control-plane check: identities, secrets, model artifacts, privileged tools, and recovery evidence.

Caveat: The post is vendor-authored and high-level; it is not an independent audit.

Follow-up: Draft a one-page training/runtime threat model with explicit recovery owners.
