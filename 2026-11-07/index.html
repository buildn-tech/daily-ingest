From adoption to operating evidence

Today’s signals are less about adding another agent and more about making delegated work governable, measurable, and learnable. Identity and action paths are becoming a control plane; coding agents need outcome proof; physical AI needs simulation; and frontier models are most useful when attached to repeatable tasks.
