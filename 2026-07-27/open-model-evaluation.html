Kimi K3 quickstart

WhatA 2.8T-parameter, 1M-context, native-vision model with an OpenAI-compatible API; full weights are scheduled for July 27.
Why it mattersIt is a current open-model scale event, with 16 of 896 experts active and a direct route to a comparative API trial.
Use nowRun one non-sensitive repo-analysis or document-synthesis task beside the incumbent model; retain prompts, latency, cost, and reviewer score.

Caveat: The platform requires a paid top-up to unlock access; “open” does not remove hosting, rate-limit, or operational constraints.

Follow-up: Create a 10-task, read-only evaluation pack before routing real client work.

UK AISI / CAISI preliminary cyber assessment

WhatA government-published preliminary assessment of K3’s exploit-development and autonomous-attack capability.
Why it mattersK3 scored 32% on ExploitBench and its safeguards did not prevent offensive attempts in the evaluation—material context for agent policy.
Use nowTreat it as a candidate for sandboxed reasoning and coding only; keep network access, credentials, and deployment authority outside the pilot.

Caveat: The assessment is preliminary and has a limited benchmark scope; it is a risk signal, not a general capability ranking.

Follow-up: Add deny-by-default tools and adversarial prompt checks to the evaluation harness.
