GAAP: deterministic privacy for tool-using agents

research-backedsecurity
  • Source: GAAP paper
  • Signal: v3 dated Sep 24; code is available

What it is

An execution environment combines structured plans, information-flow control, taint tracking, permissions, and persistent disclosure logs.

Why it matters

It moves confidentiality decisions outside the model and reports 100% blocking on tested disclosure attacks with about 10% utility loss.

Buildn / client use

Use the paper as a design benchmark for sensitive Buildn workflows: data item × recipient policies, tool interception, and durable disclosure receipts.
  • Guards survive malicious models
  • Permissions persist across tasks
  • Testable confidentiality claim
Caveat / next move: It protects confidentiality, not correctness or metadata; inspect the implementation before copying the guarantee.

What it is

A lifecycle view organizes agent failures across design, deployment, monitoring, and governance rather than treating safety as a prompt prefix.

Why it matters

It is useful as a client review checklist when the system spans model, tools, memory, identity, and human approval.

Buildn / client use

Turn the lifecycle headings into a one-page architecture review gate for client proposals.
  • Covers whole lifecycle
  • Maps failure to control
  • Good review vocabulary
Caveat / next move: Survey-level guidance needs system-specific tests; use it to frame questions, not as evidence of efficacy.

EU AI Act: post-August applicability baseline

current baselinepolicy
  • Source: European Commission AI Act framework
  • Signal: Current applicability baseline: Aug 2, 2026

What it is

The Commission notes that the AI Act is applicable from August 2, 2026 with exceptions, including prohibited practices and AI literacy obligations.

Why it matters

Clients shipping agentic workflows need a dated inventory of use cases, roles, controls, and evidence rather than a generic “AI policy.”

Buildn / client use

Add regulatory scope, operator training, logging, and human-oversight questions to discovery and delivery checklists.
  • Date the compliance baseline
  • Separate risk classes
  • Operationalize literacy
Caveat / next move: Rules and guidance evolve; obtain client legal review for high-impact deployments.

Private context is a permissioned capability

design leadproduct
  • Source: Exploring the design space of AI agents for human-as-the-unit
  • Signal: Human-centered privacy decisions remain a product surface

What it is

The paper studies privacy management tools that help people navigate complex decisions with AI and personal context.

Why it matters

Permission UX is where security, trust, and usefulness meet; an invisible policy engine will be hard to operate.

Buildn / client use

Design consent receipts and “why this data?” explanations into Buildn workflows that touch client material.
  • Make policy legible
  • Separate read from disclose
  • Keep users in control
Caveat / next move: The research is exploratory; validate with real operators before expanding permission prompts.
