13 November 2026 · hardware and robotics lane

Executive readout. Physical AI is acquiring a systems-level safety vocabulary: compute, sensors, operating software, AI behavior, cybersecurity, and certification preparation must be designed together. The useful takeaway for Buildn is not “buy robotics”; it is to reuse the same evidence-and-gates discipline for any agent that acts in the physical or operational world.
