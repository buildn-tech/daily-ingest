LLM Actions and Goals close the loop

measurement signalworkflow automation
  • LLM Actions: model output becomes a journey attribute
  • Goals: multi-campaign outcomes, deduplicated by people
  • Introductory credits: 100,000 for paying accounts

What it is: Models can classify intent, generate personalization, branch live journeys, and feed downstream logic; Goals evaluate multiple campaigns against one outcome.

Why it matters: It moves AI from “generate a thing” to “change a state, then see whether the intended outcome happened.”

Buildn/client use: Treat agent actions as hypotheses with outcome definitions, not as completed work merely because the model responded.

Caveat: dynamic personalization adds cost, latency, and policy surface area. Start with bounded outputs and explicit fallbacks.
  • Measure outcomes, not activity
  • Keep branching bounded
  • Price the inference loop

Follow-up: Add one outcome object to a current automation: target result, baseline, approval rule, and rollback.
