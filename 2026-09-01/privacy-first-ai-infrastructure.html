Privacy-First AI Infrastructure

The useful move is to put privacy enforcement before retrieval and observability. Local, tunable redaction can turn sensitive text into a safer substrate for agents without pretending that masking is a complete security boundary.

Signal map

Editorial lens: A practical gap after several agent-runtime days: local PII and secret handling is a prerequisite for useful memory, logs, and client data workflows.
