1 Tbps attacks rose 519% quarter over quarter

  • source: https://blog.cloudflare.com/ddos-threat-report-2026-h1/

What it is

Q2 accounted for 805 attacks above 1 Tbps, while 90.60% of attacks ended within ten minutes.

Buildn / client use

Short duration strengthens the case for pre-authorized automated controls and clear rollback paths.

Why useful now

Document which mitigations may execute without human approval.

Caveat: Large-volume figures do not predict a specific client’s likelihood; use them to test assumptions, not to create fear.

DNS attacks became the dominant vector

  • source: https://blog.cloudflare.com/ddos-threat-report-2026-h1/
  • published / updated: 2026-08-11

What it is

DNS-based attacks represented 34.3% of H1 network-layer activity; DNS floods reached 40% in Q2.

Buildn / client use

Make DNS provider health, TTL strategy, and authoritative-path monitoring part of the service map.

Why useful now

Add DNS failure and cache-staleness scenarios to the next tabletop.

Caveat: DNS changes can create their own blast radius; separate emergency controls from routine configuration.
