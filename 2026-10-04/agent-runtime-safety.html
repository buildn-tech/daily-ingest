Agent Runtime Safety + Sovereignty

The useful shift is from prompts and harnesses to enforceable runtime policy. NVIDIA’s platform now frames OpenShell as a practical, open runtime for bounded agent fleets, while Sentry sketches a hardware-level control plane. The near-term Buildn move is to borrow the policy shape without buying the stack.
