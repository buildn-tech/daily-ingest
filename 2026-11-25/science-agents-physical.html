GPT-6 designs proteins without specialized biology models

strong signalresearchOpenAI
  • openai.com · Nov 24, 2026 · preprint + paper PDF
  • Designs protein structures/sequences using only reasoning + code tools
  • Lab tests with Onava: evidence of binding across five targets; redesigned antibodies retained binding

What it is. An internal research model matched or beat specialized protein-design models on many tasks, guided by prompting on shape and binding constraints — no bespoke biology pipeline in the loop for design itself.

Why it matters now. The "general model absorbs the tool stack" pattern has now hit a hard-science domain with physical validation. Expect the same argument in materials, chemistry (MedChemBench, Nov 20), and other bench sciences next.

Buildn / client use. The transferable claim for any client science work: the competitive moat is moving from owning a specialized model to owning validated lab feedback loops. Also a strong proof point in pitches about agents producing testable, evidence-bearing artifacts.

  • Reasoning replaces tool orchestration
  • Binding validated in real labs
  • Promptable design constraints
Preprint, vendor-authored; "matched or outperformed on many tasks" is not "all tasks." Read the paper before quoting numbers.

Simulusion: DreamerV3, IRIS and Genie world models in PyTorch

try todayopen source
  • github.com/ParamThakkar123/simulusion · Show HN Nov 24
  • Unified PyTorch implementations of classic world models

What it is. One repo collecting runnable implementations of the world-model canon (DreamerV3, IRIS, Genie) so researchers and hobbyists can compare them on the same footing.

Why it matters now. World models keep showing up in robotics and embodied-agent coverage (serving pressure, sim-to-real). A single comparable codebase lowers the cost of actually experimenting instead of citing papers.

Buildn / client use. Low-stakes way to build real world-model literacy before clients ask; also useful for simulation-heavy prototyping in physical-product engagements.

  • World-model canon, one repo
  • Comparable on equal footing
  • Runnable, not just citable

worth exploringevals
  • kradle.ai · Show HN Nov 24 · multi-agent evaluation in a live game world

What it is. A benchmark and platform running multi-agent teams inside Minecraft — long-horizon, cooperative, physically-grounded tasks with measurable outcomes.

Why it matters now. Embodied-agent evaluation keeps shifting toward live environments where cooperation and long horizons break agents. Minecraft remains the cheapest sandbox that forces both.

Buildn / client use. Inspiration for multi-agent evals that don't need a robot lab: any shared simulated environment can stress coordination before production.
