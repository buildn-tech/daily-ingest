Physical-Agent Evaluation

The actionable lesson from robotics is broader than robotics: success must be measured at the system boundary. RLE-Bench evaluates engineering workflows and physical constraints; CLE evaluates whether experience compounds. Both are useful templates for high-stakes client automation.

Solid boxes = practical stages · green = action or evidence · amber = review point

Signal map

Physical AI is a deliberate coverage gap after several software-agent runs. RLE-Bench and CLE together show how to evaluate agents on heterogeneous artifacts, constraints, and learning over time.

  • Choose a bounded workflow
  • Keep evidence attached
  • Pause before irreversible action

01 · RLE-Bench: Can Your AI Engineer a Robot?

  • source: https://seas.harvard.edu/news/can-your-ai-engineer-robot

What it is

Harvard and Georgia Tech’s open benchmark covers 48 tasks across control, policy, perception, and mechanical design.

Buildn / client use

Borrow capability profiles instead of one “agent score” for complex delivery work.

Why useful now

It supplies a concrete design or evaluation primitive instead of another generic capability claim.

Caveat: Simulated tasks are not real-world deployment evidence.

Follow-up

Define four capability slices for a Buildn workflow.
