Agentic Delivery Surfaces

The useful unit is no longer the chat turn. GitHub is packaging agent work as resumable, code-defined delivery flows with explicit checkpoints, parallelism, and a focus on completed work. Buildn can borrow the primitives without copying the product surface.

Solid boxes = practical stages · green = action or evidence · amber = review point

Signal map

A clear product shift is making orchestration, session continuity, and task-shaped interfaces first-class; this is new practical signal after recent runtime-focused coverage.

  • Choose a bounded workflow
  • Keep evidence attached
  • Pause before irreversible action
