What it is

Boundary failures often arise when cooperating agents hold private state and infer each other’s intent.

Why it matters

A verification surface should show state handoffs and unresolved assumptions, not only the final answer.

Follow-up

Caveat: More observability can expose sensitive data; apply least-privilege logging.
