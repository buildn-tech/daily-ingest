Think Once, Reflect Twice

fresh paperHCI
  • arXiv: 2610.19991 · paper ↗

What: A study of how feedback shapes human–AI collaboration, focusing on when people receive signals and how they revise their reasoning.

Why it matters: A “human in the loop” is not enough. The interface must create a useful intervention point before the user commits to an incorrect direction.

Buildn/client use: Add checkpoint prompts to delegated jobs: what changed, what evidence supports it, what would falsify it, and what action is reversible?

Caveat: reflection prompts can become ritual; tie them to observable state changes.
  • Put feedback before commitment.
  • Ask for disconfirming evidence.
  • Record the revision.
