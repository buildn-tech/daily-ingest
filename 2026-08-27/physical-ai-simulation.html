Daily Ingest Dossier — Physical AI: Simulation Becomes Agent-Callable

Robotics is moving from model demos toward agent-executable simulation workflows. The useful near-term move is not building a robot; it is turning complex validation steps into bounded, inspectable tools.

Executive readout

The practical frontier is a tool layer between 3D assets, sensor simulation, physics, and deployment: agents can prepare experiments, but humans still approve reality-facing changes.

Signal map: solid boxes are workflow stages; green marks the practical handoff point.

What: NVIDIA added Omniverse libraries to its Agent Toolkit, including sensor simulation, GPU physics, and CAD-to-SimReady skills.

Why it matters: It makes physical-AI preparation composable instead of a manual specialist queue.

Caveat: NVIDIA-specific tooling and hardware create lock-in; treat it as an adapter boundary, not the product core.
