Browser Agents + Unit Economics

The useful shift is from “can an agent click?” to “what is the cheapest reliable loop?” Asana reports a 76× model-cost reduction and 5× speed improvement in browser tests by separating planning from execution and routing work to a smaller model. Treat browser automation as a benchmarked production surface, not a demo.

Selection rationale: A fresh Oct 9 case study gives a measurable cost/latency signal for browser agents, a practical gap after recent coverage focused on transport and sandboxing.
