AI Science Workbenches + Auditable Artifacts

Claude for Life Sciences and Claude Science position the model as a workbench connected to scientific platforms, compute, and domain skills. The transferable design pattern is an auditable artifact chain: question → tool call → analysis → draft → expert review. That is immediately relevant to research-heavy client work.

Selection rationale: Science tooling is under-covered and offers a concrete pattern for Buildn: connect domain tools, compute, and artifact provenance instead of shipping a generic chat layer.
