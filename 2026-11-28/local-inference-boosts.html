AMD RDNA patches: ~2x prefill on existing llama.cpp setups

Try itLocal inferenceAMD
  • source: devnull.land/amd-specific-llama-cpp · HN 443 pts
  • repo pattern: minimal patch set on pinned llama.cpp commit
  • prefill: ~700 → ~1300 tok/s · token gen: ~35 → ~55 tok/s
  • delivery: podman image (ghcr.io/stew675/llama-cpp-rdna-boosts)

What happened. A maintained patch set against a pinned llama.cpp commit roughly doubles AMD RDNA prefill and lifts decode ~57%, shipped as a ready podman image. HN discussion surfaced a whole ecosystem of per-architecture forks (gfx906, RDNA3) with similar or larger gains, plus the note that upstream llama.cpp is now under NVIDIA stewardship and moves conservatively — hence forks.

Why it matters. If local inference runs on any AMD card — for private client data, offline demos, or cost — this is throughput you already own. Prefill speed is the bottleneck for long-context agent work, so this directly improves RAG-heavy local loops.

  • Free 2x prefill on AMD
  • Prefill gates long-context agents
  • Fork ecosystem per architecture
Verify your GPU generation is covered (RDNA3/4 for this fork; gfx906 needs different patches) and benchmark with your own model/quant before trusting the headline numbers.

45°C inlets end the chiller era — compute cost curve drops again

ContextInfrastructure
  • source: theregister.com SC26 coverage · HN 288 pts
  • 45°C (113°F) water inlets now viable for new builds
  • result: chillers (capex, maintenance, power) drop out of design

What happened. Chips tolerant of 45°C inlet water make chiller-free datacenter designs standard for new builds, removing one of the most expensive cooling layers.

Why it matters. Every layer of the self-hosting cost stack keeps thinning — weights, kernels (above), and now facilities. The marginal case for "run it on hardware we control" improves each quarter; client conversations about on-prem AI should assume this trajectory.

  • Self-host economics keep improving
  • Cooling was a hidden capex wall
  • Assume the trend, don't buy yet
