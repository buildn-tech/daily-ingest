OpenAPI generation in the open

useful nowdirect source
  • source: https://developers.googleblog.com/why-client-sdk-generation-belongs-in-the-open/
  • decision lens: evidence → boundary → action

What it is. Google and Speakeasy open sourced a deterministic generator for typed SDKs, agent-friendly CLIs, and documentation MCP servers.

Why it matters. Generate stable client surfaces and live schema access for Buildn workers instead of hand-maintained wrappers.

  • Make the control observable
  • Keep the task contract portable
  • Leave a reviewable artifact
Caveat: AGPL applies to modifications of the generator compiler; review licensing and generated-code policy.

Follow-up: Run the generator against one small Buildn-owned OpenAPI spec and compare maintenance cost.
