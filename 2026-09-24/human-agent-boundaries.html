Design Principles for Human-Agent Interaction

useful nowdirect source
  • source: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20630v1
  • decision lens: evidence → boundary → action

What it is. A CMU position paper synthesizes 14 principles across expectation-setting, interaction, long-term adaptation, and failure repair.

Why it matters. Turn the principles into a Buildn review rubric for transparency, initiative, memory, and recovery.

  • Make the control observable
  • Keep the task contract portable
  • Leave a reviewable artifact
Caveat: Position paper plus heuristic evaluation; use it to guide tests, not as a performance benchmark.

Follow-up: Score one existing workflow against the 14 principles and log the largest gaps.

What it is. The HAI paper argues systems should explain purpose, limits, likely failures, and appropriate scope before use.

Why it matters. Make the first-run experience state what the agent may do, cannot do, and when it asks.

  • Make the control observable

  • Leave a reviewable artifact
