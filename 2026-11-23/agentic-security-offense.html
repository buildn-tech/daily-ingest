Merkle Tree Certificates reach draft -08

StandardsPKI
  • draft-ietf-plants-merkle-tree-certs-08 · updated 2026-11-01
  • Authors: Google/Cloudflare engineering (Benjamin, O'Brien, Westerbaan, Valenta, Valsorda)

What it is. The PLANTS working-group draft integrating Certificate Transparency into certificate issuance itself: CAs certify by logging, clients verify inclusion proofs, and certificates shrink dramatically — important as post-quantum signature sizes explode.

Why it matters. Certificate size and transparency are the quiet constraints under TLS for the next decade; the draft maturing to -08 means this is becoming real infrastructure to track.

Use. Mostly watch-item, but relevant to any client running large PKI estates or worried about PQ migration timelines.

Caveat. Years from deployment; ecosystem work still pending.

Action. Skim the abstract and size-comparison section once; revisit when clients mention PQ certs.
