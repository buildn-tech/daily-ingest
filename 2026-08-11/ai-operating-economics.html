ChatGPT Business Premium seats

capacity segmentationcheck economics

What: A $125 monthly Premium seat offers 5× Standard usage and removes the five-hour limit; teams can mix seat types.

Why: Capacity segmentation is a more disciplined alternative to upgrading everyone.

Use: Model client teams by workload intensity, not title; reserve high-capacity seats for artifact-producing operators.

  • Spend follows demand
  • One workspace retained
  • Admin sees usage
Caveat: compare seat cost with actual workflow value; do not treat capacity as outcome.
