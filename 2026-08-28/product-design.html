Product Design: Context Is the New Handoff

Figma’s August 26 workflow lab supplies unusually concrete design-to-code practice; the lane is selected to turn yesterday’s editable-creative signal into an implementation discipline. The practical read: convert the signal into a small, inspectable experiment rather than a broad platform bet.

Editorial readout: The common thread is operational leverage: context, controls, and evidence make fast automation safer to adopt.
