Developer Delivery: Agents Need Better Boundaries

Fresh primary-source releases make agent-generated changes more reviewable and production routing more programmable; this is a practical Buildn/client delivery gap after yesterday’s physical-AI and incident-security coverage. The practical read: convert the signal into a small, inspectable experiment rather than a broad platform bet.
