Deployment Security: Turn Findings into a Loop

A current Next.js disclosure plus an all-plan security dashboard creates an immediate, actionable security lane distinct from yesterday’s agent-incident theme. The practical read: convert the signal into a small, inspectable experiment rather than a broad platform bet.
