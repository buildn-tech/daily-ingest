2. Google signs EU AI Act Transparency Code of Practice

What it is
Google signed the EU code on transparency of AI-generated content and points to C2PA and SynthID as interoperable provenance/watermarking mechanisms.
Buildn / client use
Add a client-content delivery field for origin, editing method, approval owner, and retained source assets—especially for generated visual or audio work.

Why useful now: Customer-facing agents and AI-generated media are becoming operationally visible. This lane connects the action surface, the evidence surface, and a realistic discovery workflow for client work.

Caveat: A watermark or label supports transparency but does not establish factual accuracy, copyright clearance, or audience comprehension.

Follow-up action: Add C2PA/SynthID compatibility to the next AI-media vendor evaluation.
