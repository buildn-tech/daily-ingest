Daily Ingest Dossier — Evidence-First Agent Research

The November 19 arXiv stream is unusually practical: agents are learning to edit workflows, route computation, expose provenance, and recover from failure. The common constraint is not imagination; it is whether a claimed result can be audited and reproduced.

Signal map

The useful seam is not the feature alone; it is the handoff from signal to accountable action.

Editorial thesis: Treat research agents as experimental systems: every improvement needs a held-out replay, provenance, non-regression gate, and an explicit abstention path.

Selected signals

GUARD and ARISE: verified workflow evolution

TRYRESEARCH

What it is: The Nov. 19 cs.AI listing highlights regret-guided workflow edits with non-regression gates and enterprise workflow improvement results.

Why it matters: It frames agent improvement as controlled change management rather than unrestricted self-modification.

Buildn/client use: Turn Buildn procedures into versioned graphs with baseline scores, proposed edits, and rollback.

Why now: The new-submission stream reports measurable gains alongside fewer calls and tokens.

Next action: Apply a non-regression gate to one report-generation workflow before changing its prompt or tool sequence.

Caveat: Listing summaries are not a substitute for reading the individual paper and reproducing its setup.

Semantic context handoff

TRYMEMORY

What it is: The listing reports semantic handoff improving SWE-bench pass@1 while reducing tokens and wall-clock time.

Why it matters: The unit of memory becomes a compact, task-relevant handoff rather than a full transcript.

Buildn/client use: Pass evidence pointers, unresolved questions, and acceptance criteria between Buildn workers.

Why now: The reported result combines quality improvement with materially lower context cost.

Next action: Design a handoff envelope and compare it with transcript forwarding on ten replay tasks.

Caveat: Transferability outside coding tasks is unproven; measure omission and conflict rates.

Governed experiment ledgers

TRYGOVERNANCE

What it is: New work in the listing reports that governed ledgers capture trials that success-only logging omits.

Why it matters: A successful output is not enough to explain which paths were tried, rejected, or silently failed.

Buildn/client use: Make every Buildn experiment receipt include hypotheses, runs, failures, artifacts, and decision owner.

Why now: The research stream connects auditability to reproducibility across scientific and enterprise agents.

Next action: Add a “discarded paths” field to the next agent evaluation run.

Caveat: Ledger overhead can become noise unless fields are typed and tied to a decision.

Safe Personal RSI and bounded self-improvement

WATCHSAFETY

What it is: The listing reports a self-improvement setup that rejects risky updates and uses repair to reduce attack violations.

Why it matters: Self-improvement needs a policy boundary, rollback, and an independent check—not just a better score.

Buildn/client use: Separate proposal from activation in agent skills, prompts, and tool permissions.

Why now: The paper stream shows concrete rejection and safe-completion metrics rather than capability claims alone.

Next action: Prototype a shadow mode where proposed changes are evaluated but never activated automatically.

Caveat: Safety metrics are benchmark-specific and can miss distribution shift.

Video feedback closes the sim-to-real gap

WATCHROBOTICS

What it is: The new-listing summary reports that physical video feedback removed 72% of early robot failures in RoboBuilder.

Why it matters: Closed-loop evidence beats simulation confidence when the action changes a real environment.

Buildn/client use: Borrow the pattern for agentic workflows: verify the external state after each consequential mutation.

Why now: This is a fresh cross-domain signal that complements yesterday’s embodied evaluation coverage without repeating its papers.

Next action: Add post-action state checks to one browser or deployment workflow and classify recovery cases.

Caveat: The reported result is from a specific benchmark and may not predict field reliability.
