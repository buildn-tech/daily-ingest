Remembering Has a Price: Learning Memory Worth under Resource Constraints

fresh researchresource-aware

What: The paper treats memory selection as a value decision under limited resources.

Why now: Long-running agents cannot retain every observation without increasing latency, cost, and retrieval noise.

Use: Attach a retention reason, expected future task, freshness, and deletion rule to durable memories.

Caveat: learned worth scores can encode the system’s current task bias.
